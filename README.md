## 👋 About BDBONIK

**BDBONIK** is an e-commerce SaaS platform built for businesses in Bangladesh. It lets anyone open a professional online store in minutes — without coding and without hiring a developer — and then run the whole **order-to-delivery journey** from a single dashboard: products, orders, payments, couriers, customers, stock and marketing.

- 🌐 Website: [bdbonik.com](https://bdbonik.com)
- 🚀 Merchant app: [app.bdbonik.com](https://app.bdbonik.com/register)
- 📍 Bangladesh

---

## 🎯 The problem we solve

Most small businesses in Bangladesh start on Facebook. Orders arrive in Messenger, get copied into notebooks or spreadsheets, and are booked with couriers by hand. Cash on delivery is the norm, and **every fake order costs a delivery charge and a return**.

When these sellers want a real website, they usually have two options: an expensive custom build, or a global platform priced in dollars that doesn't speak to local couriers, mobile wallets or district-wise delivery charges.

**BDBONIK closes that gap** with a store builder designed around how Bangladeshi commerce actually works.

## 🧭 How it works

```
1. Sign up      →  app.bdbonik.com/register  (about 2 minutes)
2. Verify       →  confirm your email
3. Create store →  Your stores → Create a store → name + store address
4. Add products →  Products → Add product
5. Set up       →  payments, delivery charges, courier, theme
6. Sell         →  share your store link and start taking orders
```

**Step by step**

1. **Sign up** at [app.bdbonik.com/register](https://app.bdbonik.com/register). Enter your email, accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and follow the on-screen steps — you will be asked for your name, mobile number and other basic details.
2. **Verify your email** using the message BDBONIK sends you.
3. **Log in** at [app.bdbonik.com/login](https://app.bdbonik.com/login) — enter your email, press **Next**, then your password.
4. **Create your store** — go to **Your stores → Create a store**. Enter your business name (e.g. *Smart Fashion BD*) and a store address using lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. Country, currency and time zone are pre-set to **Bangladesh, BDT and Asia/Dhaka**.
5. **Add products** — **Products → Add product**: name, product type, brand, description, category, price, stock and images, then **Create product**.
6. **Configure** payments, delivery charges and couriers, choose a theme, and **go live**.

## 🏗️ Platform architecture

- **Multi-store by design** — every store gets its **own database** and a **separate admin panel**, keeping each merchant's data isolated.
- **Subdomain per store** — each store runs on its own `*.bdbonik.com` address, with optional custom domains and free SSL.
- **Hosted SaaS** — no servers, plugins or updates for merchants to manage.

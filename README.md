## About BDBONIK

BDBONIK is an eCommerce, SaaS, and AI technology platform focused on helping entrepreneurs, small businesses, and growing companies create and manage professional online stores in Bangladesh.

Our mission is to simplify online business through accessible software solutions, modern technology, and practical business tools.

## Our Solutions

- Ecommerce Website Builder
- Online Store Management
- Product and Inventory Management
- Order Management
- SaaS Technology
- Artificial Intelligence
- Business Automation
- Digital Business Solutions

## Our Mission

To empower entrepreneurs with technology that makes starting, managing, and growing an online business easier.

## Our Vision

To make digital commerce more accessible and support the growth of online businesses in Bangladesh.
